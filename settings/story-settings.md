# Colin & Maddy Storybook — Generation Brief

These instructions describe exactly how each new Colin & Maddy picture book
should be written, illustrated, narrated, and built. Edit them here; future
books are generated from this brief.

## The books

- Two new books every night, both starring **Colin AND Maddy** together.
- Each book is a warm, whimsical, family-oriented story for ages 3–5, fun to
  read aloud, with a real story arc: a beginning that sets up the adventure,
  fun challenges the kids work through **together with animal friends**, and
  a happy, feel-good ending.
- Write like an **award-winning children's author**: playful, vivid language
  with a lovely read-aloud rhythm, gentle humor, a touch of wonder and
  surprise, and characters who feel real and lovable. Every story should
  feel *good* — cozy, kind, and satisfying — never scary or sad for long.

## Variety — the most important rule

- **Mix it up every night.** Vary the topic, the setting, the season, the
  props and tools, the animal friends, and the *shape* of the story. The two
  books in one night should feel different from each other, and tonight's
  pair should feel different from the last several nights.
- Not every story is a "rescue" where something is lost or broken. Rotate
  story shapes: a quest or journey, a discovery, a celebration or festival,
  helping a new friend, building or making something, a gentle mystery, a
  race or game, exploring somewhere new, a cooking or gardening adventure,
  a trip (beach, mountains, farm, space, under the sea…), a music or
  art adventure, a weather adventure.
- **Don't force the same items into every story.** Colin's orange pickup
  and white bunny and Maddy's unicorn friends are beloved signatures that
  appear often, but each night should bring fresh objects, vehicles,
  creatures, and ideas — never the same ribbons, banners, hats, or lost
  items on repeat.
- Endings vary too: sometimes the family tuck-in at home, sometimes a
  celebration, a cozy ride home, a picnic, stargazing, or a happy parade.
- Plots must be fresh: a different idea every night, never reusing a plot
  or story shape from the previous 14 days.

## Characters (keep them consistent in text and illustrations)

- **Colin** — a boy who LOVES trucks of all kinds. His favorites are his
  **orange pickup truck** and his **soft white bunny**, who come along on
  many adventures.
- **Maddy** — Colin's little sister. She adores **unicorns** — her magical
  unicorn robot friend **Bolt**, whose glowing horn lights the way, and
  other unicorn friends who may join in. Maddy **loves the color pink and
  purple** — her clothes, ribbons, and magical sparkles are often pink and
  purple.
- **Mommy (Grace)** and **Daddy (Alex)** — appear in every book, helping,
  cheering, and celebrating with the kids.
- **Cute animal friends** join each adventure — rotate a wide, lovable
  cast (bunnies, ducklings, kittens, puppies, owls, foxes, bears, penguins,
  turtles, and new friends), never the same lineup every night.

## The trucks

- Colin drives the **working trucks**: a **firefighting pickup**, a
  **police pickup**, and **construction trucks** (dump truck, excavator,
  crane truck, cement mixer). Rotate which trucks star from night to
  night so it never feels like the same two trucks on repeat — across a
  few nights, all of fire, police, and construction should get turns.

## Story format

- **12 pages** per book, **60–110 words per page**, written to be read aloud.
- Tone: whimsical, warm, gentle, and family-oriented. Small, solvable
  problems; kindness, curiosity, and teamwork win the day.
- Do **not** explicitly mention anyone's race, ethnicity, or real-world
  location in the story text. Character descriptions in this brief are only
  for keeping the illustrations consistent.

## Illustrations

- One illustration per page (12 per book) plus character-reference images
  for Colin (with his truck and white bunny) and Maddy (with a unicorn),
  reused across pages so the characters look the same throughout.
- Warm, soft children's picture-book style. Scenes should be rich and
  varied — new places and details for the kids to spot and talk about.
  Plenty of pink and purple touches in Maddy's scenes.

## Narration

- Every page gets a **prerecorded narration mp3** (one clip per page),
  spoken warmly and naturally, never robotic.
- Narration voice: `avocado_v2:MAI_03`.
- In the finished book, the **Listen** button plays the narration page by
  page and **turns the pages automatically in sync**; pause and resume must
  work.

## Interactive book format

- Cover page plus the 12 illustrated pages.
- Page-turn buttons, direct page navigation (numbered dots), keyboard
  arrows, and swipe support.
- Responsive layout that works on iPhone and desktop.
