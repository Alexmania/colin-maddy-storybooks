# Colin & Maddy Storybook — Generation Brief

These instructions describe exactly how each new Colin & Maddy picture book
should be written, illustrated, narrated, and built. Edit them here; future
books are generated from this brief.

## The books

- Two new books every night, both starring **Colin AND Maddy** together.
- Each book is a warm, whimsical, family-oriented story for ages 3–5, fun to
  read aloud, with a real story arc: a beginning that sets up a friendly
  problem, obstacles the kids overcome **together with animal friends**, and a
  warm resolution that ends with the family tuck-in at home.
- Plots must be fresh: a different idea every night, never reusing a plot
  from the previous 14 days.

## Characters (keep them consistent in text and illustrations)

- **Colin** — a boy who LOVES pickup trucks. He always has his **orange
  pickup truck** and his **soft white bunny** with him.
- **Maddy** — Colin's little sister. She always has her **magical unicorn
  robot** (Bolt), whose glowing horn lights the way and helps solve problems.
- **Mommy (Grace)** and **Daddy (Alex)** — appear in every book, helping and
  celebrating with the kids, and tucking them in at the end.
- Animal friends join each adventure and help with the rescue.

## The trucks

- Colin drives a **firefighting pickup truck** in one book each night and a
  **police pickup truck** in the other book. Both are always his orange
  pickup. Alternate which truck leads from night to night.

## Story format

- **12 pages** per book, **60–110 words per page**, written to be read aloud.
- Tone: whimsical, warm, gentle, and family-oriented. Small, solvable
  problems; kindness and teamwork win the day.
- Do **not** explicitly mention anyone's race, ethnicity, or real-world
  location in the story text. Character descriptions in this brief are only
  for keeping the illustrations consistent.

## Illustrations

- One illustration per page (12 per book) plus character-reference images for
  Colin (with the orange truck and white bunny) and Maddy (with her unicorn
  robot), reused across pages so the characters look the same throughout.
- Warm, soft children's picture-book style.

## Narration

- Every page gets a **prerecorded narration mp3** (one clip per page),
  spoken warmly and naturally, never robotic.
- Narration voice: `avocado_v2:MAI_03`.
- In the finished book, the **Listen** button plays the narration page by
  page and **turns the pages automatically in sync**; pause and resume must
  work.

## Interactive book format

- Cover page plus the 12 illustrated pages.
- Page-turn buttons, direct page navigation (numbered dots), keyboard
  arrows, and swipe support.
- Responsive layout that works on iPhone and desktop.
